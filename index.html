How we work

An incubation service for ideas that need to become real.

We are an interdisciplinary agile team. The process is collaborative and adapts to each client, but a new product usually moves through four stages.

Shape the idea

We sit with you and turn the concept into something specific: what it does, who it serves, and what has to be true for it to work. You leave with a scope you can argue with.

Design and architect

Interface design and technical architecture happen together, so the thing you can see and the thing underneath it agree with each other. We choose boring technology where boring is right.

Build in the open

Short cycles with working software at the end of each one. You see progress continuously rather than at a hand-over, which is when scope problems are cheapest to fix.

Run it in production

Deployment to cloud, monitoring, and the support arrangement that keeps it healthy. Launch is the start of the work, not the end of it.

A short client list is a deliberate choice. We take on work where senior attention actually changes the outcome.

didici

Multi-location business analytics, SaaS

didici is a cloud analytics platform built for businesses running many locations. When performance data is spread across sites and channels, anomalies get masked — a problem at one branch disappears into the aggregate. didici pulls those sources into a single view, flags the areas performing outside expected control levels, and forecasts how planned changes will affect results, so teams can concentrate on the handful of sites that need attention.

Aavshyak Corporate Services

Statutory compliance, governance and risk

Aavshyak delivers statutory compliance for Indian employers: end-to-end audits against applicable labour laws, registrations and licensing under CLRA, EPF, ESIC and the Factories Act, and ongoing compliance management including liaison work when inspections arise. The wider practice covers technology-based governance, risk and compliance for organisations ranging from large corporates to start-ups.
